Privacy Policy
Restaurant Sales & Expenses

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The short version. Your sales, expenses, menu, prices and restaurant name stay on your phone. They are never sent to us. We have no server, no account system, and no way to see your business records.

The app shows advertisements, and Google's advertising software does collect some information about your device. That is described below, because it is the one place data does leave your phone.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter:

  • Sales, including items, quantities, prices, discounts and platform commission
  • Expenses, including vendors, amounts and categories
  • Your menu and its prices
  • Your restaurant's name and settings
  • Your app PIN, if you set one — stored only as a one-way cryptographic verifier, so not even the app can recover it

This is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If you want a copy, use Backup — see below.

Android's own automatic cloud backup is switched off for this app. Many Android apps are copied to the user's Google Drive automatically in the background; this one is not, deliberately, because that would send your business records off your phone without you asking. The only copy that ever leaves this device is a backup or export you create yourself.

Diagnostics: crash reports and usage counts

If the app stops unexpectedly, it records technical details so the problem can be found: the error, your app version, your Android version, your device model, free memory, and a short list of which screens you had opened.

These reports stay on your phone. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Before anything is recorded, it passes through a filter that removes business values — amounts, prices, item names, vendor names, your restaurant's name and your PIN. Usage counts record only that something happened, for example that a sale was saved, never what it was worth.

Both can be turned off in Settings → Security → Diagnostics. If you turn them off, nothing is recorded at all.

Separately, Google Play may collect crash reports from Android itself if you have opted into sharing diagnostics with Google on your device. That is a setting on your phone, not in this app, and you can change it in your Android settings.

Advertisements (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads software collects and shares information automatically, and we cannot see or control what it collects. According to Google's own documentation, it collects and shares:

  • Your IP address
  • How you interact with advertisements — app launches, taps, video views
  • Diagnostic information — app launch time, hang rate, energy usage
  • Device and account identifiers — your Android advertising ID, app set ID, and identifiers related to a signed-in Google account where applicable

Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is encrypted while being sent.

This is the only information that leaves your phone because of this app, and none of it is your business data. Google's advertising software has no access to your sales, expenses, prices or restaurant name.

You can limit this:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Choose non-personalised advertisements where the law where you live requires us to ask. In those regions the app shows a consent choice before any personalised advertisement, and you can change your answer later from the privacy options in the app. In regions with no such requirement — India included — no consent screen appears, and the limits above still apply.
  • Remove advertisements entirely by subscribing to Restaurant Pro.

Google's own policy explains what they do with it: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

Restaurant Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment entirely. We never see your card number, bank details, billing address or any other payment information — only whether your subscription is currently active, which Google tells the app so it can unlock the paid features.

Manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store app. Google's payments policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Backups and exports

The app can write a backup file, or export your data as a spreadsheet or a PDF. You choose where these go — your phone's storage, Google Drive, an email, or anywhere else your phone offers.

Two things worth knowing:

  • A backup file contains all of your business data, and it is not encrypted. Treat it like a paper ledger: anyone who has the file can read it.
  • Once you share it, what happens to it is up to you and whoever you shared it with. We have no involvement and no ability to retrieve or delete it.

The app deletes its own temporary copies after a backup or export finishes.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications — for the daily reminders, if you turn them on. Reminder text never contains any figures, so a notification on your lock screen reveals nothing about your takings.
  • Nothing else is asked of you. The app requests no contacts, no location, no camera, no microphone, and no access to files outside the ones you explicitly pick.

Google Play also lists some permissions that Android grants automatically and never prompts you about — internet access, the advertising identifier, in-app billing, fingerprint unlock, and a few used by Google's own libraries to schedule background work. They are required by those components, and none of them gives anyone access to your business records.

Children

The app is a business tool for adults and is not directed at children.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not have a server that stores your business data.
  • We do not have user accounts, and you do not give us an email address.
  • We do not sell or share your business data with anyone, because we never receive it.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

A note on security, stated plainly

The optional app lock (PIN or fingerprint) controls access to the app. It does not encrypt your records. It will stop somebody who picks up your unlocked phone from browsing your sales. It will not stop somebody determined who has your phone and the right tools.

We would rather say that clearly than describe the app as "secure" and let you assume more than is true.
